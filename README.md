# Aleksandra Gabryszak

NLP researcher at DFKI, working on evaluation and applications of large language models.

My current work focuses on:
- factuality and epistemic commitment in LLMs
- biomedical relation extraction
- evaluation of generative AI systems
- AI-assisted editorial workflows and compliance checking

## Selected projects

### BioRelFact
Biomedical relation and factuality detection, including the BioRelFact dataset and evaluation setup.

I am the first author of the accompanying LREC 2026 paper:

**From Facts to Hypotheses: Joint Detection of Biomedical Relations and Epistemic Commitment Using LLMs**

[Repository](https://github.com/DFKI-NLP/biomed-relation-factuality-detection)

### GenKI4Media
Research on generative AI assistants for editorial workflows, including:
- compliance with journalistic standards
- length-constrained summarization
- evaluation of AI-assisted editing
- citation attribution and factuality
